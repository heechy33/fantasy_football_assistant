## Pre-declared Phase 2c gates (fix must satisfy all three)

The fix is chosen from FFC-only fitting (Phase 2a: H2 per-player CV transfer first; H1 band-aware
kernel second; one-parameter monotone recalibration last). Against this baseline:

1. **Human Brier improves.** New human analytic pooled Brier < 0.0220 AND new human all-seat pooled
   Brier < 0.00540 (strictly lower on both).
2. **Bot Brier does not regress.** New bot analytic pooled Brier <= 0.0207 + 0.0005 AND new bot
   all-seat pooled Brier <= 0.00433 + 0.0005 (0.0005 absolute tolerance, ~0.5 Brier points).
3. **Section C regret / top-choice agreement does not regress.** New mean plan regret <= baseline
   + 0.5 utility points AND top-choice agreement >= baseline - 5 percentage points, on both the
   bot and human cohorts.

Any gate failing means the fix does not ship; the experimental label stays and the current model is
carried to draft day (per the approved Phase 2c decision rule).
