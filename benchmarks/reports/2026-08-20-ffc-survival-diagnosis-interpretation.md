# FFC survival-curve diagnosis — interpretation (Phase 2a)

Hand-authored addendum to `2026-08-20-ffc-survival-diagnosis.md` (that file is regenerated by
`pipeline/survival_diagnose.py` each run; this one documents the meaning of the numbers and what
they imply for Phase 2c). Data: `fixtures/ffc/adp-ppr-observed.json`, pinned FFC PPR board, window
2026-08-13→20, 6,978 twelve-team mocks, 264 players (263 scored).

> **Correction (2026-08-21):** the original version of this doc read the deep-tail band's raw
> asymmetry as "left tail longer (reaches dominate)" and recommended a band-flipping H1 kernel. That
> reading did not survive a right-censoring check added after the fact (`right_censoring`/
> `h1_tail_asymmetry_excluding_censored` in `survival_diagnose.py`): FFC's feed is a fixed 15-round
> mock (max pick 180), and 58% of deep-tail players have `low` within 10 picks of that ceiling —
> their observed right tail is truncated by the mock's length, not by real drafting behavior.
> Excluding those rows flips the deep-tail asymmetry from **+3.96 (left-longer)** to **−0.26**
> (essentially symmetric, mildly right-longer) — consistent with the other three bands. The
> corrected finding below replaces the original; see the module docstring in
> `survival_diagnose.py` for the full mechanism.

## Findings

**H1 — kernel skew is real and (once corrected for right-censoring) consistently right-tailed, not
band-dependent.** Under normality `(adp−high) ≈ (low−adp)`; it does not hold. The raw pooled table
by band:

| Band | mean (adp−high) | mean (low−adp) | raw direction | near-ceiling fraction | asym excl. censored |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <=12 | 3.62 | 5.04 | right tail longer | 0% | -1.42 (unchanged) |
| <=24 | 8.46 | 9.46 | right tail longer | 0% | -1.00 (unchanged) |
| <=48 | 12.22 | 14.11 | right tail longer | 0% | -1.89 (unchanged) |
| deep (>=48) | 35.48 | 31.52 | left tail longer (**raw, artifact**) | 58% | **-0.26 (corrected)** |

The shallow three bands have 0% of players near the mock's ceiling, so their right-tail-longer
reading was never in question. The deep-tail band's raw left-tail-longer reading is a right-
censoring artifact (see the correction note above): once the 125 near-ceiling players are excluded,
the remaining 90 honest observations agree with the rest of the board — right tail longer, roughly
symmetric to mildly right-skewed. The pooled number (left 30.7 vs right 27.7, before correction) was
a Simpson's-paradox artifact of the censored subset of the deep tail dominating the count, not a
real reversal.

**Meaning for the model:** in every band, the true distribution has a *longer right tail* than the
normal kernel with the same mean/stdev — players fall more than Φ predicts. `1 − Φ((pick−adp)/stdev)`
therefore **understates survival** (overstates the chance a player is already gone) across the board,
including in the deep tail, where the earlier draft showed the opposite sign only because of the
mock's fixed length. **The fix (H1), if it ships, should be a single right-skew-dominant kernel, not
a band-flipping one** — the data no longer supports flipping direction for late-round players, and
doing so would make bench/late-round availability worse, not better. The top band's left tail is
additionally mechanically bounded by pick 1 (33% of <=12 players have `high==1`), a separate, expected
floor effect that does not change this conclusion.

**H1 — range vs normality: mild, does not reject.** Observed `(low−high)` is ~0.92× the
`d2(times_drafted)·stdev` a normal predicts (median ratio 0.92, pooled; only 2% of players >1.2×).
This is consistent with FFC's `high/low` being genuine min/max picks with slightly thin tails — not
a reason to reject the model by itself, but it agrees with the skew finding that the normal kernel
is the wrong shape.

**H2 — within-band heterogeneity is real, strongest at the top.** Observed per-player CV
(`stdev/adp`) vs the band constant used by `fitted_stdev`:

| Band | band CV | median obs CV | p10–p90 | p90/p10 | band CV representative? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <=12 | 0.247 | 0.218 | 0.177–0.380 | 2.14 | yes (but 2.1× spread) |
| <=24 | 0.169 | 0.170 | 0.139–0.206 | 1.47 | yes |
| <=48 | 0.124 | 0.122 | 0.100–0.161 | 1.62 | yes |
| deep | 0.112 | 0.112 | 0.083–0.160 | 1.93 | yes |

The band CVs are good *central* estimates (median observed CV tracks them within ~0.01) and the
floor still protects the very top. But the top band has a 2.1× p90/p10 spread — two players at the
same ADP can have materially different dispersion — so the band constant flattens real per-player
structure there. `mean observed/fitted stdev` is 0.90 in the top band (fitted overstates top-band
spread by ~10%) and ~1.01–1.07 elsewhere, including the deep tail (1.065) — i.e. even with the deep
tail's `low` values right-censored, `fitted_stdev` is not overstating deep-tail dispersion; if
anything the true deep-tail stdev is probably slightly higher than observed, since a censored `low`
can only pull an observed `stdev` down, never up. This does not change H2's guidance, but it means
H2's per-player CV transfer is a safe floor for the deep tail rather than a risk in the same
direction as H1's original band-flip error.

## Implication for Phase 2c

> **Update (2026-08-21): H2 was implemented and gate-checked — it does not ship.** Applied to the
> currently-committed `data/adp-ppr.json`/`data/adp-espn-ppr.json` and rerun through the benchmark,
> it improved the bot cohort (analytic Brier -0.000451, all-seat -0.000088) and left Section C
> planning quality unchanged, but *regressed* the held-out human cohort on both metrics (analytic
> +0.000272, all-seat +0.000025) — failing the "human Brier must strictly improve" gate. See
> `DECISIONS.md`'s 2026-08-21 entry and `2026-08-18-availability-calibration-baseline-phase2c.md`'s
> H2 result section for the full numbers. `build_data.py` was reverted to not wire it in. H1 below
> is therefore unattempted and unvalidated, not merely "next in sequence" — H2 was the lower-risk
> option per the ordering below, and it failed.

Both H1 and H2 are "identified," so the plan's repair menu applies with the following guidance
(revised after the right-censoring correction above):

1. **H1 kernel replacement**, if it ships, should be a **single right-skew-dominant kernel applied
   across bands**, not the band-flipping (right-skewed early/mid, left-skewed deep) kernel originally
   guided here. The deep-tail band's apparent left-skew does not survive excluding the 58% of players
   whose `low` sits within 10 picks of the mock's 180-pick ceiling; the corrected deep-tail asymmetry
   (-0.26) agrees in direction with the other three bands. Re-run `survival_diagnose.py`'s
   right-censoring check against whatever FFC snapshot is live at implementation time before
   finalizing the kernel — the fixture's `total_drafts`/window changes weekly and could shift the
   exact magnitude.
2. **H2 per-player CV transfer** is simpler and band-local: carry the FFC-observed CV ratio
   `stdev/adp` onto the Sleeper/ESPN mean instead of the band average, keeping the floor and a
   shrinkage/tolerance guard. It does not fix the skew by itself, and is unaffected by the
   right-censoring finding (see the H2 note above).
3. **Neither conclusive → one-parameter monotone recalibration** is the weakest candidate, and is now
   more plausible than originally stated: with the skew corrected to a single right-tail direction
   rather than band-flipping, a monotone map is no longer structurally ruled out the way a
   band-flipping skew would rule it out. Still a fallback only, not the first choice.

**Sequencing per the Phase 2c gate doc stays H2 first, H1 second** — H2 can proceed unchanged; H1
must incorporate the right-censoring exclusion before any kernel is chosen, not fit against the raw
deep-tail number in the original (now-corrected) table above.

The ESPN human drafts (Phase 2b, `espn_draft1.txt`/`espn_draft2.txt`, both 10-team all-human) are
the arbiters per the Phase 2c gates — fit on FFC only, validate there, and ship nothing that fails
any gate.
